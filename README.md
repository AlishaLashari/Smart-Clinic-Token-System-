### 📍 Problem in Interior Sindh Clinics
In Dadu, Larkana, Sehwan clinics, 30 patients gather around compounder shouting "Mera number kab ayega?". Doctor gets disturbed. Patients fight.

### ✅ Solution
This app runs on **ONLY 1 MOBILE** (Compounder's mobile).

1. Patient comes -> Compounder clicks "NEW TOKEN" -> Gives chit with number (e.g., Token 12)
2. Patient waits OUTSIDE, not at desk
3. Big display on TV/Mobile shows: **NOW: 5 | WAITING: 10**
4. Doctor finishes patient -> Compounder clicks "NEXT" -> Speaker says "Token 6 andar aao"

### 🔥 Features for Pakistani Clinics

- **1-Mobile Model** - No need for patient app, works on compounder's phone only
- **Big Display** - 60px font, visible from 20 feet / Connect to TV
- **Offline Works** - SQLite database, no internet needed after install
- **Daily Hisab** - Auto calculates total patients & kamai (Rs.500 x patients)
- **Voice Ready** - Ready to connect Bluetooth speaker for announcements
- **Urdu Mix UI** - "Naya Token Do", "Agla Mareez Bulao" - Compounder understands
